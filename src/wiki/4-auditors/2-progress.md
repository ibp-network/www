# Programme history

The short timeline of how the IBP became what it is today. For per-cycle audit detail, see the curator bounty reports linked from the multisig on-chain.

## 2022: Kusama bounty launches

**Early 2022.** [Kusama Referendum 35](https://kusama.polkassembly.io/referenda/35) funds the IBP for an initial cycle. Stake Plus (`us-east`), Helikon (`europe-southeast`), Amforc (`europe-west`), interweb (`europe-west`), Gatotech (`america-central`), Turboflakes (`europe-southwest`) join between October 2022 and February 2023.

## 2023: Geographic coverage broadens

**Spring–autumn 2023.** Dwellir (`africa-west`), RadiumBlock (`asia-south`), Rotko Networks (`asia-southeast`), Polkadotters (`australasia-east`), Lucky Friday (`us-west`) onboard. Membership peaks at eleven active operators.

## 2024: Polkadot bounty

**April 2024.** [Polkadot Referendum 649](https://polkadot.polkassembly.io/referenda/649) brings the programme onto the Polkadot treasury alongside Kusama. Curator multisig is restructured to a 3-of-5.

**Through 2024.** Paseo launches as the community's public testnet and IBP coverage pivots to it. Westend remains operational but narrows to Parity's internal testnet role; IBP retires its Westend nodes during 2025, since the public-facing testnet need is now served by Paseo. Membership operates at peak size: the eleven active members carried through from the 2023 expansion.

## 2025: Full-coverage operation

**Through 2025.** Around eleven members, active operators carrying every supported relay, system parachain, and external parachain in the IBP rotation. Curators continue quarterly audits; the staged "milestone" tier used for 2022–2023 onboarding sits dormant.

**[Polkadot Referendum 1516](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/referenda/1516)** funds the year's top-up of the original 2024 bounty: same shape as before, same membership, continued coverage of every active member.

## 2026: Leaner top-up reshapes the membership

**Early 2026.** Internal review of the next funding cycle starts. By the time the proposal is drafted, the curators and Parity reviewers agree the bounty should fund only chains the treasury has a public-good case for carrying. Chains with a healthy commercial RPC market exit the bounty rotation. Members whose service was tied to those chains exit alongside, or transition to direct-from-parachain funding (Hydration is the first).

**February 2026.** [Subsquare #443](https://polkadot.subsquare.io/posts/443) opens the discussion phase of the next top-up. It has not yet been submitted as a referendum, and the proposal has gone through several rounds of Parity / W3F feedback since. Headline changes proposed:

- Forward spend drops to ~$1.42M / year (≈ 58.68% reduction vs. the proposal's own initial draft, after Parity/W3F's cost-saving feedback).
- Six external parachains exit the bounty-funded rotation: Acala, Ajuna, Bifrost Polkadot, Nexus, Moonbeam, Unique.
- Hydration becomes the first external parachain on a treasury-of-parachain funding model: Hydration's own treasury pays IBP directly, IBP members also run a quorum of Hydration collators.
- Paseo testnet redundancy reduces to a smaller member subset; mainnet (Polkadot, Kusama) keeps full per-member coverage.
- Membership lands at __IBP_MEMBER_COUNT__ active members (__IBP_MEMBERS_LIST__). The earlier "milestone" tier is formally retired; all active members operate at the same service level.

The full rationale is in the [blog post](/blog/leaner). The proposal is on Subsquare in the discussion phase at the time of writing.
